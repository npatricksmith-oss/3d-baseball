## Summary of Findings

- **P1 (Critical — spec/rules/invariant violation or major correctness bug)**: None. `PIVOT_S_50 = 0.80 s` (`[E]`) and scaling by `Transfer` grade (`0.686 s` at grade 70 against J.J. Hardy's measured `0.67 s`) are well-defended from the public sources; `holder.rs` (`force_continuation` and `pivot_release`) reads only public positions, velocities, and teammate attributes (Invariant 2); all math uses `bb_math::m` (Invariant 4); and replayability is preserved (Invariant 3).
- **P2 (Moderate — logic bug, spec/code mismatch, or physical/modeling flaw)**:
  - **P2-1**: In `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live.rs`, an off-bag feed receiver at second (`pivot_wait_bag = true`) is blocked from throwing anywhere (`if fd.pivot_wait_bag { return; }`) even when the holder's evaluator rejects second base (`p_out <= 0.3`) and selects a throw to first, third, or home; `fd.pivot = true` applies `SetUp::pivot` (`0.80 s`, no hurry) to subsequent throws to third or home (`base != 1`); and when the force at second expires before the receiver gets the bag (`f.pivot_wait_bag && !force_at_second`), `f.face` and `f.target` are not reset to an ordinary receiver's state.
  - **P2-2**: Unassisted carries to second base (`6U-3` / `4U-3`, `f.carry_to == Some(2)`) reset the carrier's set-up clock on touch (`f.up_at = f.up_at.max(t)`) and charge the full `0.80 s` catch-to-release pivot time (`SetUp::pivot`), discarding the transfer time already spent while running with the ball, creating a `1 cm` cliff at the `0.6 m` touch radius, and contradicting `docs/agent-models.md` §5.6 (`line 469`).
  - **P2-3**: `pivot_release` (`arriving = true`) and `docs/agent-models.md` §5.5 assume a covering pivot man arrives across the bag facing his approach (`> FACE_MIN_MPS`) and that a second baseman taking a feed while moving toward second owes `+0.2 s` footwork (`AWAY_FOOTWORK_S`). In `live.rs`, `Role::Cover(2)` brakes to a stop on `base_pos(2)` (`want = sqrt(2·BRAKE·d)`), so at the `0.6 m` touch radius his speed is `2.683 m/s < FACE_MIN_MPS (3.0 m/s)` (causing `live.rs` to use the feed direction `-bv` while `pivot_release` uses `approach`), and from Savant's `y = 42.5 m` depth a 2B's velocity component away from first base is at most `1.26 m/s < AWAY_MPS (2.0 m/s)`, so he never pays `+0.2 s` footwork and instead receives a `-0.10 s` glove-side turn bonus (`0.727 s` mean pivot).
- **P3 (Minor — test coverage gap)**:
  - **P3-1**: `the_holder_predicts_the_pivot_the_world_makes` only tests `arriving = false` (pivot man stationary on the bag), `a_pivot_off_the_bag_starts_when_he_gets_it` checks only a loose lower bound (`>= 0.68 s`), and there is no regression test for the `eb2812e` non-force / removed-force fix at second base.

## Detailed Findings (P1–P3)

### P2-1: Off-bag feed receivers at second are blocked from abandoning a lost force at second, `fd.pivot` locks throws to third/home, and `!force_at_second` leaves `face` and `target` dirty
- **Where**: `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live.rs:2191–2205`, `2509–2511`, `2780–2783`, `2818`, `2874–2877`, `2945–2950`; `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live/holder.rs:146`
- **Problem**:
  1. **Blocked throw when R1 is still `< 2·BASE_L − 0.1` but will beat the off-bag receiver to second**: When a feed to second (`to_base == 2 && !relay`) is caught outside the `0.6 m` touch radius while `force_pending_at_second()` is `true`, `try_captures` (`live.rs:2201–2204`) sets `fd.pivot = true` and `fd.pivot_wait_bag = true`. If R1 is close enough to second that the receiver cannot beat him to the bag (`p_out <= 0.3` for carrying to second), `holder_options` selects throwing directly to first (`o.base = 1`) to retire the batter (or to third/home on a lead runner). However, at `live.rs:2875`:
     ```rust
     if fd.pivot_wait_bag {
         return;
     }
     ```
     This early return blocks the fielder from throwing to first, third, or home (and skips `self.abandon_carry(by)`) until either he runs all the way to second base (`d < 0.6 m`, starting a fresh `0.80 s` pivot clock) or R1 crosses `2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1`. `if fd.pivot_wait_bag` should only gate the throw when the fielder actually still intends to complete the force at second before throwing, or `pivot_wait_bag` should be cleared when `best` selects a throw without a pending carry to second.
  2. **`fd.pivot` applies to throws to third (`base = 3`) and home (`base = 4`), not only first (`base = 1`)**: `docs/agent-models.md` §5.5 (`line 442`) defines the pivot as *"A force at second followed by a throw to first is a pivot, its own action and not a relay."* In `live.rs:2945–2950`, `set_up_for(by, tgt)` branches on `if fd.pivot` without checking whether `tgt` is first base (`base_pos(1)`). If a middle infielder forces R1 at second with runners on first and second (or first and third) and then throws to third or home to retire a lead runner, `set_up_for` charges `0.80 s` (`pivot_s`) and forbids hurrying (`earliest_s == set_s`).
  3. **Incomplete reset when the force expires before the receiver gets the bag (`live.rs:2780–2783`)**:
     ```rust
     if f.pivot_wait_bag && !force_at_second {
         (f.pivot, f.pivot_wait_bag) = (false, false);
         f.set_base = RELAY_TRANSFER_FRAC * f.a.transfer;
     }
     ```
     In `try_captures` (`live.rs:2191–2204`), an ordinary receiver has `fd.face = None` (no turn penalty), whereas the pivot branch sets `fd.face = Some(...)`. When `!force_at_second` clears `f.pivot` at line 2781, it leaves `f.face = Some(...)` in place (so the ordinary receiver still pays `turn_extra_s`) and leaves `f.target = base_pos(2)` (from line 2191, which `abandon_carry` at line 2936 does not clear because `f.carry_to` is `None`), so the receiver keeps running toward second base while setting up to throw elsewhere.
  4. **Threshold and retouch mismatch (`2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1` vs `2.0 * BASE_L`)**: `force_pending_at_second()` (`live.rs:2510`) and the base-touch check (`live.rs:2818`) use `r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1`, whereas `holder.rs:146` and `out_weight` (`live.rs:2493`) use `r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L` (a `10 cm` / 1-tick window where `holder.rs` predicts a pivot force at second while `live.rs` neither pivots nor touches second). In addition, `force_pending_at_second()` does not check `r.last_base < 2` (if R1 has already touched second base and retreated below `2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1` on an uncaught ball, the force at second was already removed by touching second).

### P2-2: Unassisted carries to second (`6U-3` / `4U-3`) reset the set-up clock on touch and charge the full `0.80 s` catch-to-release pivot time
- **Where**: `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live.rs:2784–2794`; `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live/holder.rs:93–99`; `docs/agent-models.md:442, 469`
- **Problem**:
  1. `PIVOT_S_50 = 0.80 s` represents the *catch-to-release* time of a pivot man receiving a feed at second base (`ball hitting glove to leaving hand`, Fagerstrom 2014). When a fielder carries a ground ball to second base himself (`f.carry_to == Some(2)`), he already secured the ball at `t_catch` and transfers it from glove to throwing hand while running to the bag. Resetting `f.up_at = f.up_at.max(t)` and setting `f.set_base = throwing::pivot_s(f.a.transfer)` (`0.80 s`) with `f.pivot = true` upon reaching `d < 0.6 m` (`live.rs:2785–2794`) discards all transfer time elapsed during the carry and forces the carrier to wait an additional full `0.80 s + turn + footwork` after touching second base.
  2. **1 cm cliff at `d = 0.6 m`**: If a fielder secures a ground ball at `d = 0.59 m` from second base, on the very first `holder_act` tick (`live.rs:2785`) `f.carry_to` is still `None` (`carried == false`), and line 2821 immediately emits `BaseTouched { base: 2, holding_ball: true }` retiring R1; he then throws to first using his normal `SetUp::new(0.65 s)` clock from `t_catch` (hurryable to `0.39 s`). If he secures the ball `2 cm` farther away at `d = 0.61 m`, tick 1 sets `f.carry_to = Some(2)` and tick 2 triggers `on_bag && carried` (`live.rs:2786`), resetting `f.up_at` to `t_touch` and requiring a non-hurryable `SetUp::pivot(0.80 s)`.
  3. **Internal spec contradiction**: `docs/agent-models.md` §5.5 (`line 442`) states that the pivot applies to *"the receiver of a throw there (the feed), or a fielder who carried the ball to the bag himself"*, whereas §5.6 (`line 469`) still states: *"A receiver needs his existing 0.7 × transfer; a carrier retains setup time already spent holding the ball."*

### P2-3: `Role::Cover(2)` braking (`2.683 m/s < FACE_MIN_MPS = 3.0 m/s`) and Savant depth (`y = 42.5 m`) break both `pivot_release`'s `arriving` facing and §5.5's 2B footwork/turn assumptions
- **Where**: `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live/holder.rs:121–133`; `sim/crates/bb_game/src/live.rs:109, 1096–1097, 2203, 2787`; `docs/agent-models.md:445`
- **Problem**:
  1. **No momentum across the bag; `FACE_MIN_MPS` vs `sqrt(2·BRAKE·0.6)` mismatch**: In `fielder_step` (`live.rs:1096–1097`), any covering fielder (`fd.role == Role::Cover(2)`, so `fd.role != Role::Chase`) has `early = true` and `target = base_pos(2)`. Instead of running *across* second base with momentum, he brakes to stop on the center of the bag along `want = sqrt(2.0 * BRAKE * d)`. At the `d = 0.6 m` force-touch radius, his speed is `sqrt(2.0 * 6.0 * 0.6) = 2.6833 m/s`.
     - In `holder.rs:126–127`, `pivot_release` assumes that when `arriving == true`, the pivot man at `d = 0.6 m` has speed `sqrt(2.0 * BRAKE * 0.6) = 2.6833 m/s` and **faces `approach`** (`face = approach`).
     - In `live.rs:2203` (`try_captures`) and `live.rs:2787` (`holder_act`), facing the direction of motion requires `m::hypot(vel.x, vel.y) > FACE_MIN_MPS`, where `FACE_MIN_MPS = 3.0 m/s` (`live.rs:109`). Because `2.6833 m/s < 3.0 m/s` (in fact `sqrt(12·d) < 3.0 m/s` for all `d < 0.75 m`), a pivot man who catches the feed at `d < 0.75 m` or steps into `d < 0.6 m` has `moving == None` in `live.rs` and faces the incoming feed `(-bv)` (or his original ground-ball catch facing), **not** `approach`.
  2. **2B never owes `+0.2 s` footwork and instead gets a `-0.10 s` turn bonus**: `docs/agent-models.md` §5.5 (`line 445`) states: *"A second baseman taking a feed from the left side while still moving toward it owes the footwork for taking the ball moving away from first (+0.2 s); a shortstop coming across the bag toward first owes none, and his turn is the straight-ahead quarter turn already inside `pivot_s`."*
     - Under Savant 2024 `DoublePlay` positioning (`2B` at `(+11.98, 42.48)` RHB or `(+9.95, 42.47)` LHB), `2B` starts `3.7 m` deeper than second base (`(0, 38.80)`), so his approach vector to second base is `(-0.956, -0.294)` (West-Southwest, with a `-Y` component *toward* first base). Its projection opposite `to_first = (+0.707, -0.707)` is `0.468` (RHB) or `0.418` (LHB). At the `d = 0.6 m` braking speed of `2.683 m/s`, `2B`'s speed away from first base is at most `0.468 × 2.683 = 1.26 m/s < AWAY_MPS (2.0 m/s)`. To exceed `AWAY_MPS = 2.0 m/s`, `2B` would need speed `> 4.27 m/s`, which under `want = sqrt(12·d)` only occurs at `d > 1.52 m` (outside `RECEIVE_REACH = 1.5 m`). Thus `2B` **never** owes `AWAY_FOOTWORK_S` (`+0.2 s`).
     - Moreover, because `turn_extra_s` (`throwing.rs:92–103`) treats `PIVOT_S_50 = 0.80 s` as already containing a `90°` quarter turn from a square stance and subtracts up to `0.12 s` when the target is off the fielder's glove (left) shoulder, a right-handed `2B` facing a feed from `SS`/`3B` (or facing West-Southwest) has his left shoulder pointed South-Southeast (`18°–25°` from first base) and receives a **`-0.09` to `-0.11 s` turn bonus** (`0.727 s` mean pivot in `dp_probe`, `sd 0.027 s`), whereas a right-handed `SS` standing on second facing a feed from `2B` has his left shoulder pointed North and receives a **`+0.05` to `+0.06 s` turn penalty** (`~0.86 s` pivot).

## Answers to Review Questions

### 1. Baseball and physics (`0.80 s` at grade 50, `Transfer` scaling, momentum across the bag, unassisted step-and-throw)
- **Is `0.80 s` defensible from the sources, and do better public measurements exist?**
  - Yes, `0.80 s` (`[E]`) is defensible and well-chosen given what is publicly available. MLB Statcast does not publish a public leaderboard for second-base pivot/turn times (unlike catcher pop/exchange times; Statcast's 2016 "Exchange Time" leaderboard, where Dustin Pedroia led 68 qualified second basemen at `1.02 s` against a `~1.2 s` league average, blends routine ground-ball fielding exchanges with double-play turns).
  - Across public sources (checked independently), the available anchors are Fagerstrom's video-timed `0.67 s` catch-to-release for J.J. Hardy on an elite 4-6-3 turn (FanGraphs, 18 Aug 2014), college/pro coaching benchmarks of `0.7–0.9 s` for the pivot turn, and `4.0–4.5 s` (`<= 4.3 s` target, Coaches Insider / Piraro) for total contact-to-first time on a completed double play.
  - Setting `PIVOT_S_50 = 0.80 s` at grade-50 `Transfer` (the midpoint of `0.7–0.9 s`) and scaling by the existing `Transfer` ratio (`×1.08` per 10 grade points via `pivot_s(transfer_s) = PIVOT_S_50 * transfer_s / INFIELD_TRANSFER_50_S`) places grade 70 at `0.80 / 1.08² = 0.686 s` (within `0.016 s` of Hardy's `0.67 s`) and grade 80 at `0.635 s`, while completed pivots in `dp_probe` average `0.752 s` (`0.753 s` on `main`) catch-to-release and `4.31 s` contact-to-first.
- **Does the model let him take the throw coming across the bag (a real pivot man's momentum)?**
  - **No (see P2-3).** A covering middle infielder has `Role::Cover(2)` (`fd.role != Role::Chase`), so `fielder_step` (`live.rs:1096–1097`) sets `early = true` and brakes him to a dead stop at `target = base_pos(2)` (`want = sqrt(2·BRAKE·d)`). If he reaches the bag before the feed (the common case when `2B` or `SS` starts `9.5–13 m` from second and the grounder takes `1.5–2.0 s`), he is standing stationary (`vel = 0`) on the bag. If he is still arriving at the `0.6 m` touch radius, his speed has already braked to `2.683 m/s < FACE_MIN_MPS (3.0 m/s)` (so `live.rs` does not even credit him with facing his direction of motion), and under Savant `DoublePlay` depth (`y = 42.5 m > 38.8 m`), `2B` approaches at `-163°` with at most `1.26 m/s` away from first base (`< AWAY_MPS = 2.0 m/s`), so `2B` never pays the `+0.2 s` moving-away footwork that `agent-models.md` §5.5 claims.
- **Does a shortstop's unassisted step-and-throw (`6U-3` / `4U-3`) get the same clock when it should not?**
  - **Yes — it gets the full `0.80 s` pivot clock from the moment he touches second, when it should not (see P2-2).** When a fielder carries the ball to second base (`f.carry_to == Some(2)`), `holder_act` (`live.rs:2785–2794`) resets `f.up_at = f.up_at.max(t)` when he enters `d < 0.6 m` and sets `f.set_base = pivot_s(f.a.transfer)` (`0.80 s`) and `f.pivot = true`. Unlike a feed receiver who first touches the ball at second base and must exchange it from glove to hand during the `0.80 s`, a carrier already fielded the ball and completed his glove-to-hand transfer while running to the bag (`docs/agent-models.md` §5.6, `line 469`: *"a carrier retains setup time already spent holding the ball"*).

### 2. Same class of error as `eb2812e` (force removed mid-play, runner already at second, tag play at second, feed arriving before the fielder)
- **Force removed mid-play (batter out first, or R1 safe before the receiver gets the bag)**:
  - If the batter is retired before the throw to second is caught (e.g., `1B` touches first base on a `3-6` reverse double-play attempt), `forced(r1)` (`live.rs:2481`) is `false`, so `force_pending_at_second()` is `false` and the receiver at second is not made a pivot man.
  - However, if `force_pending_at_second()` is `true` when the receiver catches the feed **off the bag** (`d >= 0.6 m`, `pivot_wait_bag = true`):
    1. While R1 is still `< 2·BASE_L − 0.1` (`force_pending_at_second() == true`), if R1 is about to beat the receiver to second (`p_out <= 0.3` for carrying to second) and the receiver's evaluator selects throwing directly to first (`b = 1`) or home (`b = 4`), `if fd.pivot_wait_bag { return; }` (`live.rs:2875`) **blocks the throw** until R1 actually crosses `2·BASE_L − 0.1` or the fielder touches second (**P2-1**).
    2. Once R1 crosses `2·BASE_L − 0.1` while `pivot_wait_bag` is still `true`, `live.rs:2780–2783` clears `f.pivot` and `f.pivot_wait_bag` and restores `f.set_base = 0.7 * transfer`, **but fails to reset `f.face = None` and `f.target = stop_point(f)`** (**P2-1**).
    3. If the receiver at second *does* make the force out at second (`d < 0.6 m`, `f.pivot = true, f.pivot_wait_bag = false`) and then throws to **third or home** (`b = 3, 4`) on another runner (or after the batter is safe at first), `f.pivot` stays `true` and forces `SetUp::pivot` (`0.80 s`, no hurry) on a non-first-base throw (**P2-1**).
- **Runner who has already reached second**:
  - Within `2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1 <= r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L` (the last `10 cm` before second base), `holder.rs:146` treats R1 as still forced to second (`r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L`), whereas `force_pending_at_second()` (`live.rs:2510`) and the base-touch check (`live.rs:2818`) return `false` (`r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1`). Furthermore, if R1 has already touched second base (`r.last_base >= 2`) and retreats below `2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1` on a ball in play, `force_pending_at_second()` still returns `true` because it checks `r.s < 2.0 * BASE_L - 0.1` without checking `r.last_base < 2` (**P2-1**).
- **Tag play at second**:
  - When there is no force pending at second (`force_pending_at_second() == false`), a throw to second for a tag play leaves `fd.pivot = false` and `fd.pivot_wait_bag = false`. When the runner is within `carry_max_m = 8.0 m` and visibly moving to second, `holder_options` selects the tag-plan carry to second (`carry_to = Some(2)`), keeping the receiver at `base_pos(2)` until `TagContact` within `tag_reach_m = 1.2 m`.
- **A feed that arrives before the fielder does**:
  - `receiver_ready(i, 2)` (`live.rs:2998–3001`) estimates the cover man's arrival using an accelerating standing start (`run_time(d - 0.5, 8.0, 0.9)`), ignoring both his initial velocity (if he jumped or broke the wrong way) and the braking deceleration (`want = sqrt(2·BRAKE·d)`) that `fielder_step` applies to `Role::Cover(2)` over the last `2.7 m` (`~0.18 s` slower than `run_time`).
  - As a result, the release gate (`live.rs:2917`, `race.direct < race.receiver_at - 0.05`) can release the feed before the cover man reaches `d < 0.6 m`. If the throw arrives when the cover man is `0.6–1.5 m` from second base (`<= RECEIVE_REACH`), he catches it off the bag (`pivot_wait_bag = true`), takes `0.10–0.25 s` of braked running to reach `d < 0.6 m`, and only *then* starts his `0.80 s` pivot clock (`f.up_at = f.up_at.max(t)`), whereas `holder.rs:98–99` (`force_continuation`) assumes his `0.80 s` pivot clock starts immediately at `option.ball_at` (`d.min(v).max(receiver_at) + 0.05`).

### 3. Invariant 2 (the holder's continuation and `pivot_release`)
- **Does `force_continuation` / `pivot_release` read only what a fielder can see and know?**
  - **Yes — Invariant 2 is respected.** Every input to `force_continuation` (`holder.rs:83–114`) and `pivot_release` (`holder.rs:121–133`) is either public field geometry (`fd.pos`, `pf.pos`, `base_pos(1)`, `base_pos(2)`), public runner motion (`batter.s`, `batter.v` screened by `visible_target`), the holder's own throw-time belief (`option.ball_at`, `throw_time`, `receiver_ready`), or teammate attributes (`pf.a.transfer`, `pf.a.throws`, `pf.a.arm`). A middle infielder on a team knows his partner's throwing hand, arm strength, and pivot quickness (`Transfer`). No private runner `goal`, realized throw trajectory, or future RNG draw is read.
  - Note, however, that when `arriving == true`, `pivot_release`'s prediction of `face = approach` disagrees with `live.rs` whenever the pivot man catches or reaches the bag at speed `< FACE_MIN_MPS (3.0 m/s)` (**P2-3**).

### 4. Results, arithmetic, and why `3B` (`66%` vs `38%`) and `SS` (`68%` vs `54%`) remain high
- **Arithmetic check**:
  - All arithmetic in `docs/calibration/2026-11-14-dp-pivot.md` is verified:
    - Grade-70 `pivot_s`: `0.80 / 1.08² = 0.6859 s ≈ 0.69 s` (vs. `0.67 s` Hardy).
    - Real MLB weighted infield conversion (Baumann, FanGraphs 2025): `(8.2×19.6 + 0.7×3.8 + 14.3×21.5 + 25.4×44.7 + 23.6×37.8 + 27.8×54.1) / 100.0 = 40.02% ≈ 40.0%`.
    - Sim weighted infield conversion (`eb2812e`): weighting `P 49.2%`, `C 0%`, `1B 54.8%`, `2B 38.7%`, `3B 65.5%`, `SS 67.8%` by their shares of the `74.9%` infield grounder chances gives `54.0%` (`+14.0 pp` above `40.0%`).
    - Run-environment effect: `0.61` fewer grounder double plays per game (`2.59 → 1.98`, or `~0.30` per team-game) becoming 1-out fielder's choices (`GB FC .032 → .056 [.036 → .059]`) at `~0.65` runs per out saved near 1–2 outs yields `+0.20` expected runs per team-game, matching the measured `+0.22 [+0.13]`.
- **Do `3B` (`66%` vs `38%`) and `SS` (`68%` vs `54%`) — while `2B` is *below* MLB (`39%` vs `45%`) — point to something beyond batter speed, slides, and the pitcher?**
  - **Yes.** While the fixed `4.417 s` (`sd = 0`) batter home-to-first time (against a `4.26–4.30 s` MLB mean with `sd = 0.20 s`, when completed pivots arrive at first in `4.314 s` mean) and the absence of slides (A6) lift all double plays, they act symmetrically across fielders and cannot explain why `3B` (`+27.7 pp` over MLB) and `SS` (`+13.7 pp`) are far above MLB while `2B` (`-6.0 pp`) is below MLB. Four structural factors in the sim drive the `3B`/`SS` excess and the `3B`/`SS` vs `2B` inversion:
    1. **The `2B` vs `SS` pivot turn asymmetry (`0.727 s` vs `~0.86 s`, P2-3)**: On `5-4-3` (`3B`) and `6-4-3` (`SS`) plays, `2B` is the pivot man; `turn_extra_s` gives a right-handed `2B` facing a feed from the left side a `-0.09` to `-0.11 s` glove-side turn bonus (`0.727 s` mean pivot in `dp_probe`, `sd 0.027 s`) and `0.0 s` footwork. On `4-6-3` (`2B`) plays, `SS` is the pivot man; standing on the bag facing `2B`'s feed puts first base `~130°–135°` toward his throwing shoulder (`+0.05` to `+0.06 s` turn penalty, `~0.86 s` pivot), and unassisted `4U-3` carries pay a fresh `0.80 s` after touching second (**P2-2**). That `~0.13 s` pivot handicap directly depresses `2B`-fielded double plays relative to `3B` and `SS`.
    2. **Glove-side turn bonus (`-0.08` to `-0.12 s`) and no backhand penalty on feeds from `3B` and `SS`**: For a right-handed `3B` at `(-18, 28)` or `SS` at `(-12, 42)` facing the ball/plate, second base lies `3°–31°` off his left (glove) shoulder, so `turn_extra_s` *subtracts* `0.08–0.12 s` from `Transfer` (`0.65 s → 0.53–0.57 s` set feed). Furthermore, `live.rs` models no backhand fielding or cross-body transfer penalty (`docs/agent-models.md:420`), and when a thrower hurries a feed from the hole (`SetUp::new`), `earliest_s = 0.6 * (base_s + turn_s)` (`0.32–0.39 s`) omits `footwork_s` (`0.20 s`), trading an 18% speed loss and `3×` direction `σ` (`~0.7 m` over a `12 m` feed, still well inside `RECEIVE_REACH = 1.5 m`) for up to `0.46 s` of release time.
    3. **Missing `3B` grounder share (`11.3%` of infield chances vs `23.6%` MLB) and selection bias**: In `dp_probe`, `3B` fields only `8.5%` of all grounder chances (`11.3%` of the `75.3%` infield chances, vs `23.6%` in Baumann), and `1B` fields only `5.1%` (`6.8%` of infield chances vs `14.3%` MLB), because `P` takes `20.5%` of infield chances (`15.4%` of all chances) and wide/slow rollers or hard hole grounders either go through (`30%` in `3B–SS`) or require dives (`1.5 s` get-up, which never attempt 2B). Thus the `3B` grounders that are fielded on the feet in the sim are disproportionately hard balls hit nearly straight at `3B` (`t_sec ≈ 1.2–1.6 s`).
    4. **How often real third basemen go to second at all**: In the sim, R1 runs first-to-second from a `5.5 m` lead (`21.93 m`) in a fixed `3.48 s`, and `3B`'s `0.54 s` transfer + `0.62 s` throw (`37.95 m/s` over `22.7 m`) gets the ball to second in `1.16 s` after capture, so `3B` goes to second and gets a high-probability force (`p_out ≈ 1.0`, `p_second > 0.3`) on nearly every standing grounder he fields (`1.4 s + 1.16 s = 2.56 s << 3.48 s`), whereas real third basemen fielding balls to their backhand or moving toward the line routinely take the single sure out at first (`5-3`).
