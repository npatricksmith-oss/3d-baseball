### P2-2. The slack treats a projected pickup after the bounce as a catch

The 0.30 s allowance preserves main’s literal. The stated rationale is approximate arrival timing. The actual rule gives the runner credit for reachable pickups on the post-bounce trajectory, which can order a full return to the bag even when every visible fielder is too far away during the projected flight. The named [E] value has no calibration in the change or setting on `RunnerStartParams`. The finer scan alone resolves the new regression fixture, with a 0.617 s intercept and 0.788 s projected landing when slack is zero. The catch scan also inherits a 50 ms delay before its first sample from the throw-race scan. ### P2-3. The pre-existing read of a thrown ball gets exact position and velocity

`runner_decide` and `runner_relook` can reach `ball_time_to` → `thrown_read`. This path feeds the true instantaneous thrown trajectory’s position and velocity from `ball_at` directly to the runner’s flight model. It infers a destination with typical spin and drag from those unperturbed inputs. This predates the branch and is separate from the first catch check. The other runner-facing `ball_at` calls generate the noisy loose-ball read or supply the held ball’s publicly visible holder location. The held-ball race also uses `fd.up_at`, the fielder’s exact scheduled recovery time, as the time he appears ready. Both paths are inside `bb_game` and outside the scope of the crate dependency test. ### P3-1. The low-liner test can pass without a contact decision

The fixture’s expectation is independent of the catch predictor. It checks a physical glove secure and a rules `Catch` in a specified time window, then bounds the runner’s forward speed by the given 3 m/s lead. Restoring the true-height gate alone or restoring the 150 ms scan alone made him accelerate to 3.75 m/s before the catch and failed the test. Delaying the first look to 1.0 s left the runner in `Read` at 3.0 m/s throughout the assertion window and still passed. An explicit check that he has left `Read` and begun braking before the catch would cover the new decision as well as the speed ceiling.
