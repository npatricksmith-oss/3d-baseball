# The double-play pivot (2026-11-14, `work/dp-pivot`)

**Nothing was tuned.** The pivot at second is now its own action (agent-models §5.5): the man at second with the ball and a force there to make (the receiver of the feed, or a fielder who carried the ball to the bag) has to have the bag. He then pivots in his own time, plus the §5.5 turn and footwork from how he got the bag, and has no early release. The holder's double-play estimate uses the same model. Before, the receiver of any throw at second had 0.7 × `Transfer` (0.455 s at grade 50), no turn, and the general hurry floor of 0.27 s. The sources are docs/calibration/2026-11-14-tag-ups.md §6–7 and the A4 follow-up's ranked causes (docs/calibration/2026-11-14-throw-choices.md, Follow-up B).

## Why it lands there

- **The run environment rose by 0.22 [0.13]** because about 0.3 double plays a team-game became a force at second with the batter safe at first. That is the fielder's-choice rise, worth about +0.15–0.25 runs at the run expectancy of a runner on first against two outs. A counterfactual with the pivot's structure but the old 0.455 s time gives 5.19 [5.52] runs with grounder double plays unchanged (.089 [.090]): the structure alone does nothing, and the time is the cause.
- **A bug found that way and fixed (`eb2812e`).** The first version made every receiver of a throw at second a pivot man, force or not. A cutoff or a middle infielder taking an outfield throw at second then could not throw home until he had touched the bag. That added 0.3–0.4 runs with no change in double plays. Now a feed to second is a pivot only while a force there is still to be made.
- **Still 14 points above the real conversion.** By fielder, the excess is largest where the real game turns the fewest: the pitcher (49 against 20), the first baseman (55 against 22) and the third baseman (66 against 38). The second baseman is below (39 against 45).
  1. *The batter is slow to first in the sim:* home to first takes 4.417 s for every batter, against 4.26–4.30 s measured (tag-ups note §1, §7). That gives the defense 0.12–0.16 s more on every double play, about a fifth of the pivot's 0.75 s, and it is the one remaining cause with a measured value. It is the runner's model (the tag-ups note's τ fit, 0.68 s), left to its owner.
  2. *No slide.* A real pivot man is hurried and disrupted by the runner from first; the seam is at nothing (A6).
  3. *The pitcher.* See below.
  4. *Second baseman against shortstop.* §5.5's turn rule gives a second baseman facing a feed from the left side a throw to his glove side (turn −0.10 s), and a shortstop standing on the bag facing the second baseman's feed a throw to his arm side (+0.06 s). So second-base-fielded balls (the shortstop pivots) convert least. The real game also has the shortstop-fielded ball converting more (54 against 45), but by less. Whether the two pivot men really differ that way is unsourced; not tuned.
- **The feed (fielder's glove to release on the throw to second): 1.05 s on old main, 0.89 s on `f3dc029`, 0.74 s after.** It is `Transfer` plus the §5.5 turn and footwork, plus any wait for the man covering second. The drop after the change is a selection effect: the probe times only feeds that went on to a throw to first, and a slower pivot drops the slow-feed attempts. No feed time was found in the sources, so nothing was changed.
- **The pitcher fields 20% of infield double-play chances (14.7 of 74.9% of all chances) against 8.2% real, and converts 49% against 19.6%.** After his delivery he reacts like any infielder. Only a dive waits for `PITCHER_RECOVER_S` (0.7 s); a pitcher's post-delivery reaction and range are not modeled, and no source gives them. Part of the real figure is sacrifice bunts, which the sim does not have. Not changed: nothing here has a measured value.

## Tests

- `live::tests::a_pivot_man_on_the_bag_lets_go_after_his_pivot_time`: 0.80 s plus the spec's turn for facing the feed. It holds with about 0.16 s in hand, where the general rule would hurry the throw.
- `live::tests::a_pivot_off_the_bag_starts_when_he_gets_it`.
- `live::tests::the_holder_predicts_the_pivot_the_world_makes`: the holder's predicted pivot equals the world's to a tick, and the relay at first is within 0.15 s of his estimate.
