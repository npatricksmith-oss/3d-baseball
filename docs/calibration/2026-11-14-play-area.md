# The limits of the play area (owner backlog A10, 2026-11-14)

**Nothing was tuned.** The park now has a backstop, foul-territory walls (the front of the stands) and dugouts (physics-spec §9.5). A ball that leaves the field is dead, and the play ends at that fact. A thrown ball out of play gets the two-base award, from the time of the pitch or of the throw (rules-spec §7.2). Before, foul ground reached the outfield fence's 330 ft in every direction: a foul pop could be chased 100 m from the plate, and no throw could ever leave the field.

The reference park's geometry comes from sources where there are any: the backstop distance from OBR 2.01, and a side clearance that makes foul territory match the major-league average area. Nothing about it was chosen for its effect on the run environment.

## The park (physics-spec §9.5)

| | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Backstop | 60 ft behind the apex, 9.0 m screen | OBR 2.01 recommends 60 ft or more; screen height [E] |
| Side walls | 50 ft off the foul lines, 4 ft high | [E]; with the taper below, this gives the MLB-average foul area |
| Taper | from 100 ft along the line to the foul pole | [E] |
| Dugouts | openings in the side wall 20–80 ft along each line | [E], unsourced |
| Foul territory inside the walls | 23,885 sq ft | MLB average ≈ 23,300 (Beyond the Box Score, 2019); Oakland ≈ 40,700 |

## Why each number moved

- **Foul outs fell by 1.6 a game, and foul balls out of play rose by 10 a game.** Foul pops that used to be caught 30–100 m from the plate now leave over the stands wall, into the dugouts, or over the backstop screen. MLB had about 1.4 foul outs a game in 2017 and 1.8 in 2003 (FiveThirtyEight, "Foul balls are the pace of play problem nobody's talking about": season totals 3,450 and 4,372 over 2,430 games). The sim's 2.1–2.3 is still high, but much closer than 3.7–3.9 was.
- **Fouls rose by 2.2 a game:** those same pops are now fouls instead of outs.
- **PU share fell from 11% to 9% of balls in play**, and every other type's share rose a little. A caught foul pop counts as a ball in play (a pop-up out); one in the seats is a foul. Hit rates within each type did not move beyond noise: FB .127 → .137 and PU .015 → .021 move in opposite directions on the two seed sets.
- **Reached on BIP rose by 1.1–1.4 points, from composition alone.** Taking 1.6 always-out balls a game out of about 55.6 balls in play gives 18.07 / 54.0 = 33.5%. Measured: 33.6–33.9%.
- **K% rose by 0.5–0.8 and pitches by 5 a game.** A pop that was the last pitch of a PA is now a strike, and the PA goes on. **PA/team-game rose by 0.4** because there are fewer outs.
- **Runs/team-game rose by 0.15 and 0.35 on `a69dd50`** (0.20 and 0.39 against the walls-out-of-reach build), **and by 0.01 and 0.09 on `52d6632`.** The four pairs average +0.15. The outs alone predict about +0.22: 0.8 fewer outs per team-game, at about 0.28 runs per out avoided in this run environment. The thrown-ball awards add a little more, perhaps +0.03: 0.05–0.085 per team-game, each worth two bases to the runners on. The `a69dd50` pairs' average, +0.25, matches that; the `52d6632` pairs' +0.05 falls short of it by about 1.3σ, and the four together (+0.15) sit inside the noise of the +0.25 expected. The gap between pairs is noise: a 300-game mean has σ ≈ 0.13 runs/team-game, and the two builds diverge after their first changed play, so they are independent samples.
- **HR/PA rose by 0.14 and 0.43 points on `a69dd50`, and by 0.04 and 0.12 on `52d6632`** (mean of the four +0.18). Some rise is expected: PAs that used to end on a foul pop go on to other outcomes, about 5% of them home runs. That accounts for only about +0.1 point. Against the walls-out-of-reach build the rise is 0.17 and 0.40, about 2σ together (σ ≈ 0.15 points per run). **Unexplained beyond that:** I have no mechanism except the extra PAs. The smaller rise on `52d6632` points to noise, but that is not settled. It is listed in the ledger's §5.
- **Throws out of play: 0.10–0.17 a game**, all of them new. No MLB rate was to hand. Errors run about 0.5 per team-game and throwing errors about half of those, and only some throwing errors leave the field. This rate is plausible but unchecked.

The run environment is further from MLB's ≈ 4.5 than before (5.41 and 5.62 on main as merged). Per the brief, nothing was tuned to pull it back.

## Not done

- No protective netting above the side walls: a ball that would strike it goes into the seats. With netting to the far end of the dugouts, fewer foul pops would reach the dugouts.
- The outfield fence still meets the ball at its center, not its surface (3.7 cm off). A rolling ball still stops dead at any wall, as it did at the fence before.
- There is no glove-deflection fact yet. The engine has the "fair fly deflected out over foul ground" award (OBR 5.05(a)(8)), but the sim cannot produce that play.
- The client: `bb_ffi` does not decode the `BallDead` marker yet (data-formats §5.4). The client needs its time and exit point (in `BbPlayInfo`) before it can cut back to the field.
