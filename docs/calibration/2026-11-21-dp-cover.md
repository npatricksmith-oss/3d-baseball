# The covering man and the receiver's estimate: saved plays 66 and 168 of seed 51001 (2026-11-21)

**Job.** Build the two fixes the [saved-plays diagnosis](2026-11-20-saved-plays-51001.md) §1 decided for the owner's plays 66 and 168 (no double-play attempt). Branch `work/dp-cover`, from main `667762a`. Spec: agent-models §5.3 (the covering man's movement) and §5.5 ("The covering man and the receiver's estimate", the pivot).

## 1. What was built

1. **The receiver's estimate from his visible motion** (`receiver_ready`, `cover_reach`). A teammate judged a covering man ready when a run *from rest* at league-typical speed (8 m/s, τ 0.9 s) brought him to 0.5 m from the bag. It now steps the movement itself from the man's visible position and velocity and his known speed, by the same rule the world moves him with, until he is within receiving reach (`RECEIVE_REACH`, 1.5 m); at most 4 s (`COVER_STEP_CAP_S` [E]), the rest at top speed. Where a force is pending it is timed to the throw being weighed.
2. **How a covering man takes a force base** (the note's Q-A, option (b); `cover_step`). With a force pending at his base he brakes onto the bag only when he is early for the throw: his time to it (the chaser's form) leaves `EARLY_MARGIN_S` = 0.1 s [E] (the chaser's literal, now named) before the throw by his belief. Otherwise he comes at it to cross it at `COVER_CROSS_MPS` = 4 m/s [E, unsourced], his speed toward it held to √(v_c² + 2·`BRAKE`·d). Within the touch radius, or past the bag, he brakes onto it. Covers with no force there keep the stop.
3. **His belief of the throw** (`cover_feeds`, `feed_seen`). With the ball in a teammate's hands: as the holder would throw with time in hand, from what is seen (his catch, a dive's get-up or a stretch, the turn and plant his catch left him, his known `Transfer`, `pivot_s` for a feed at second, his known arm). With the throw in the air to him: his own read of it (`receiver_read` now returns when his read has it at his spot). With the ball loose: none (he brakes, as before).
4. **A force is the touch.** A throw option to a force base is timed to the later of the ball's arrival, the man's reach and his coming within the touch radius (0.6 m, now `BAG_TOUCH_M`), by the same stepping; the double play's second force at first too. The pivot's estimate (`pivot_release`) takes the velocity he takes the bag with (braking or crossing) instead of the fixed 2.39 m/s. A man crossing the bag moving away from first owes the plant (agent-models §5.5).

**Two corrections found while measuring** (both on this branch, before the final runs):
- *Ready at reach is not the out.* With the force option timed at the reach (1.5 m), the holder's estimate and a carry (timed to the touch radius) were compared on different terms. Item 4 fixes it.
- *Crossing before a throw that is not coming.* The first belief counted only the holder's transfer. In seed 23 play 268 the first baseman took the ball diving and was still getting up (1.5 s): the covering pitcher, believing the toss due, crossed first at 4 m/s, caught it 1.5 m past the bag and lost the batter. In 60 games, batters safe on a throw the pitcher caught near first went from 1 (main) to 9; with the belief of item 3, 1.

## 3. The Opus review's fixes (2026-11-21, [review](../reviews/2026-11-21-opus-dp-cover-review.md))

- **P1-1, the hold.** With the receiver estimate exact, the old release rule (the ball may reach the bag up to 0.05 s *before* the man is within 1.5 m of it) let throws go to arrive just as a man coming from the side reached 1.5 m; they passed out of his reach. The pitcher's feeds to second not taken by their receiver went 0 of 66 (main) → 39 of 115 (seeds 0–255; seed 12 play 105, frozen as `12-105.json`: the feed rolled into centre field and the runner took third). Now a man not yet within reach counts as ready `RECEIVE_MARGIN_S` = 0.1 s [E, unsourced] after he gets there, and the holder waits while his throw would reach the bag before that.
- **P3-2.** The holder timed the cover's crossing to his own hurried release, while the cover moves by his own belief (the holder's set throw): a man who in fact braked was believed up to 0.12 s sooner than he came. The holder now works the cover's belief out as the cover does (`feed_seen`, from what the cover sees of him): belief against world within 0.03 s (`a_feed_held_for_a_cover_coming_across_is_taken_by_him`, starts 4–10 m out at 0–7 m/s, every feed taken by the man at the bag).
- **P3-3 (third gap).** A holder visibly running the ball to the cover's bag (faster than 3 m/s, within about 25° of the line) is carrying it: the cover times no throw from him and brakes onto the bag.
- **P3-1, P3-4.** The world's force-touch, pivot-start and pivot-wait radius is `BAG_TOUCH_M`; the `PIVOT_FACE_MIN_MPS` comment names both arrivals.
