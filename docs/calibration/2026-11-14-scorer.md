# Official scorer and B5 audit, 2026-11-14

Owner backlog A12 (box score) and B5 (the fielder's-choice call). Rules-spec §10–§11; code in
`bb_stats::scorer` and `bb_stats::box_score`; captions in `bb_game::describe`.

## What the scorer is

A pure function of the event log (invariant 6). `bb_stats::scorer::score_play` reads one
`PlayRecord` and the outs before it and returns the batter's ruling (hit of 1–4 bases,
fielder's choice, out, strikeout, walk, hit by pitch, reached on a thrown-ball award, or
undetermined), a sacrifice-fly flag and the RBI. `box_score` runs it over a whole log and builds the
per-player and team batting lines (PA, AB, R, H, 1B/2B/3B/HR, RBI, BB, K, HBP, SF, LOB).
`sim-cli game` prints them.

The scorer decides a batter's hit or fielder's choice (OBR 9.05) from what the log carries:

- **Preceding runner out.** A force, or an infielder's putout of a runner advancing one base
  or returning, denies the hit (9.05(b)(1), (3)). A tag on an extra-base attempt does not.
- **Unsuccessful play on a preceding runner.** The defense tried to put out another runner
  and failed; the hit is denied if an ordinary throw to first would have retired the batter
  by `choice_margin_s` (0.15 s, `ScorerOptions`, rules-spec §10.1 `m_fc`).
- **The ordinary-effort counterfactual** is `ScorerEvidence.clean_play_margin_s`: the batter's
  actual first-base touch minus the arrival of a **noise-free reference throw** of a set,
  grade-50 fielder of that position, from the first secure possession, in the actual
  atmosphere. `bb_game::game::reference_throw_time` computes it after the play from the
  world's facts and writes it to the log. It never reaches `bb_agents` (invariant 2). A throw
  that cannot reach first on the fly continues through a noise-free bounce; one that never
  reaches the bag has a margin of negative infinity; `has_margin` tells "none recorded" from zero.

## Error seam, and what is provisional

The sim has **no fielding errors**, and the scorer rules none. It invents none (A17 will add
them from physical causes). The seam is one named outcome:

- `Outcome::ReachedOnThrowAward`: bases given for a thrown ball that left the field
  (OBR 5.06(b)(4)(G)). It is an at-bat without a hit, with no RBI for a run the award itself
  scores. This is the case the sim already contains. When A17 lands it becomes *reached on
  error*. Without it the award was counted as a hit worth its bases.
  (The A10 play-area work, merged the same day, created these plays.)

Provisional, each stated in rules-spec §10.2:

- The batter's goal at a throw to another base stands in for whether an extra base was taken on
  that throw; a full counterfactual of his running is not available.
- The sacrifice-fly boundary is a 45 m radial approximation of the outfield
  (`outfield_radius_m`), not a park-specific line.
- A walk-off that ends before the batter's first-base touch leaves the hit **undetermined**,
  not guessed: 0 of 300 games in seeds 0–299, 2 in seeds 1000–1299.
- Logs written before the evidence existed give undetermined rulings, not wrong ones.

## B5 audit (300 games per seed set, current main `69826e4` plus this branch)

`cargo run --release -p bb_stats --example scorer_audit -- 300 <first seed>` plays each game
with presentation on, compares the old lab classifier (`classify_bip`: it reads facts the log
does not hold and makes none of the scorer's judgments) with the scorer on every ball in
play, checks I1, I2 and I7 on every team of every game, and prints sample captions. These are
scorer judgments over simulated facts, not hand-reviewed video.

| | seeds 0–299 | seeds 1000–1299 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Plays the old classifier called a fielder's choice | 449 | 426 |
| — scorer calls a **hit** | **15** (3.3%) | **28** (6.6%) |
| — scorer agrees, fielder's choice | 426 | 394 |
| — other (reached on a thrown-ball award, or out) | 8 | 4 |
| Plays the old classifier called a hit that are now a fielder's choice | 74 | 70 |
| Plays the old classifier called a hit that are now a thrown-ball award | 26 | 24 |
| Unresolved rulings (walk-offs) | 0 | 2 |

MLB is about .245 and .295. Nothing here was tuned: the scorer reads the game, and
the high averages are the sim's own (the CLI's reached-on-BIP and GB hit-rate numbers already showed them).

**Why the 15 and 28 are hits.** Every one is an infield ball where another runner was put out
on the bases and the old rule denied the hit for *any* runner put out after an infielder had
the ball. The scorer applies 9.05(b)(1), (3), (4) as written: a runner thrown out *taking an extra base* does not cost the
batter his hit. The 65–74 the other way are failed plays on a preceding runner where an ordinary throw
to first would have got the batter (a fielder's choice by (a)(6), (b)(4)), which the old
classifier did not judge.

### Captions

The caption names what happened. From the logs (seeds 0–299 and 1000–1299):

- Still a fielder's choice: "Reached on a fielder's choice to second, runner out at third";
  "Reached on a fielder's choice to short, runner out at second, 1 run scores".
- Was a fielder's choice, now a hit: "Single to short, runner out on the bases".
- Was a hit, now a fielder's choice: "Reached on a fielder's choice to the pitcher".
- Thrown-ball award: "Reached on a throw out of play, bases awarded (ball hit to second)".
- A runner is tagged out and the batter did not reach: "Ball hit to the pitcher, runner
  tagged out at home". A walk-off with the hit unruled: "Ball hit to short, 1 run scores".

## Accounting identities (rules-spec §11), tests over many games

`bb_stats/tests/accounting.rs`: 80 games (seeds 1000–1079) for the team identities, 40 seeds for the pitch and runner identities; the audit example also checks I1, I2 and I7 on 600 games:
I1 box-score proof (written PA = R + LOB + putouts, the form of the spec's identity once AB + BB + HBP + SF + SH = PA), I2 PA = AB + BB + HBP + SF + SH, I4 and I5 (outs and batters faced against the
pitcher's side), I6 line score and runs, I7 H = 1B + 2B + 3B + HR, I8 pitches, I10 every runner
in a live ball ends once (out, scored or left). The players' lines sum to the team's.
Scorer cases are built by hand from OBR 9.05 and 9.02 examples
(`bb_stats/tests/scorer_cases.rs`), not from game output.

## Gameplay unchanged

The log now carries `ScorerEvidence` (first handling, first secure possession, base touches,
throws with origin, target and release, carries, the reference-throw margin) and advance
causes. No agent reads them. Live.rs only **emits** these facts.

Compared against main `69826e4` with `a12_gameplay_compare` (hash of every pitch's record,
rules events and rules facts, run on both builds): **seeds 0–299 and 1000–1299, 600 games,
identical streams, run totals and line scores.** Seeds 0, 1, 7, 42 (runs 4–6, 7–6, 7–3, 8–7)
match as well. The event-log **digests do change**, because the play records hold the new
facts: that is the only difference. The standard run on this branch equals main's recorded row: 5.41 [5.62] runs, K% 16.3 [15.8],
HR/PA 5.18% [5.09%], GB .353 [.353].
