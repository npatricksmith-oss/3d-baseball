# Hurried throws and the turn to throw (owner backlog A3, 2026-11-13)

**Nothing was tuned.** Fielders now need time to get set for a throw, can let go early at a cost in speed and accuracy, and turn further to throw off the throwing shoulder than off the glove shoulder (agent-models §5.5). The new parameters are estimates [E]; this note records what they did.

## What a game looks like (`cargo run --release -p bb_game --example throw_probe -- 300 0`)

| Throws | a game | taken in the air by another fielder |
|---|---|---|
| set | 46.3 | 96.3% |
| hurried, up to 1/3 | 0.29 | 96.6% |
| hurried, 1/3 to 2/3 | 0.29 | 96.5% |
| hurried, over 2/3 | 1.96 | 92.5% |
| on balance | 34.1 | 96.3% |
| off balance (owed footwork, whether or not he took it) | 14.8 | 95.7% |

- **About 2.5 throws a game are hurried**, most of them fully: a fielder either has the time or he does not. The second baseman hurries most (mean hurry 0.10: the double-play relay), then the shortstop and first baseman.
- **A fully hurried throw is not taken cleanly twice as often** (7.5% against 3.7%). "Not taken in the air" includes throws that are meant to bounce (beyond the arm's carry), so 3.7% is not an error rate.
- **Three throws in ten are made by a man who took the ball moving away from his target** (mostly outfielders). Almost all of them take the 0.2 s to get set.

## The run environment moved (seeds 0–299, `sim-cli games --n 300 --seed 0`)

| Build | runs/team-game | reached on BIP | GB hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (handedness, `a4c8219`) | 5.35 | 32.0% | .346 |
| A3 | 5.54 | 32.5% | .352 |
| A3 without footwork (0 s moving away and after a dive) | 5.17 | 31.0% | .337 |
| A3 without the turn (`TURN_S` 0) | 5.59 | 32.6% | .349 |
| A3 never hurrying (`SAFE_MARGIN_S` below any margin) | 5.57 | 32.6% | .354 |
| all three off | 5.35 | 32.0% | .346 |

- With all three off the build reproduces the old numbers exactly: moving throw noise into `motor::execute_throw` changed no game.
- **Footwork costs the defense 0.37 runs**; hurrying and the turn give back 0.18 between them. The footwork was in the spec before but had not been built. It lands mostly on outfielders' throws, where 0.2 s is a runner's extra 1.6 m.
- One seed set only (the per-variant differences of 0.03 to 0.05 are inside its noise; the footwork's 0.37 is not).
- **Open for the next calibration** (with the handedness recalibration): runs are now 5.54 against MLB's about 4.5. The outfield's interim transfer of 0.85 s (against 0.75 in §10) may already have stood in for some of this footwork; check it against measured outfield exchange times before leaning on either number.
