## Findings stated in the abstract
- The swing starts with a weight shift toward the rear foot and the generation of trunk coil.
- During the stride, the force applied by the front foot is 123% of body weight, and it promotes segment acceleration about the trunk axis.
- Kinetic link order: the hip segment peaks first (714 deg/s), then the shoulder segment (937 deg/s).
- Maximum linear bat velocity is 31 m/s.
